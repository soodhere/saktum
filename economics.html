What the model says

Annual recurring revenue by product line, 2027 to 2031. Amounts are company finance in US dollars; on the platform itself every price is in prepaid tokens.

    How the model works

    1. Public MCP servers grow each year from today's measured count.
    2. A share of them is checked with Saktum Check (free), rising from year one to year five.
    3. Some teams upgrade to the Team tier; some of those publish behind Saktum Gateway; companies running agents adopt Saktum Guard in proportion.
    4. Gateway, Guard and Verified start after Trust Check 1.0 (from April 2027), so 2027 revenue is mostly the Team tier. Enterprise contracts ramp from 2028. Saktum Verified certifies a share of Gateway publishers. Saktum Exchange (take rate) and Saktum Bounty (programme fees) start in 2028.
    5. Costs are headcount at a fully loaded cost, plus other operating costs and cost of revenue. Runway uses the 2027 burn.
