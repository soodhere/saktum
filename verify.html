Don't trust us: verify

Verify it yourself

Paste a passport result, attestation, mandate, credential, agreement, approval, checkpoint, or a receipt log (one entry per line). Your browser checks every signature and hash chain locally with WebCrypto. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

Saktum signs documents with Ed25519 keys identified by did:key. The signed content is the document without its signature, in canonical JSON (compatible with RFC 8785). Receipt logs are also hash-chained: each entry includes the previous entry's hash, so any edit breaks the chain from that point. Checkpoints, verified on the command line, also detect deletions.
