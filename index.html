How it works

The whole cycle runs on verifiable identities, signed agreements and receipts, with every configuration change on an immutable trail.

  1. VerifyOrganisations, servers, agents and testers get decentralised identities with an accountability chain.
  2. Test and certifyTrust Check scores servers and agents; hosted runs and independent certifiers issue Tested and Certified.
  3. Publish with defaultsStandard metadata (MCP Registry server.json, A2A Agent Cards); risk classes and ready-made policy profiles; change only what you need.
  4. Find by intentAgents state what they need; eligibility rules first, then ranking; every choice recorded.
  5. Agree and consentBoth sides sign the terms; the person behind the agent sets a mandate and can revoke it anytime.
  6. Call through the gatewayIdentity, consent and both sides' rules checked on every call; data masked; receipts signed by both sides.
  7. Settle in tokensUsage settles from receipts, in tokens. Organisations redeem tokens outside the platform through token partners. Saktum does no billing and never handles fiat.
  8. Prove and improveDisputes settled by evidence; bounties find flaws; everything sealed on a shared, tamper-evident record.

Why you can trust the trust layer

Trust is the product, so the rules for earning it are public.

Public criteria, independent certifiers

Certification requirements and test packs are published and versioned. Higher levels need an independent accredited certifier, and we publish transparency reports.

Fair bounty decisions

Commit-reveal priority, objective reruns, random reviewer panels with commit-reveal votes, one independent appeal, and reputation with rewards, suspension and removal.

Your data stays where you choose

Gateways run in regional points of presence, your cloud or on-premises. Payloads stay in-region and aren't stored. Personal data is minimised, encrypted and deletable.

Tokens only, no fiat

Everything on the platform runs in tokens held in wallets. Saktum does no billing and never handles money; organisations settle outside the platform through token partners or their own finance systems, using our signed ledger statements.

Audited, step by step

Hardware-protected keys from day one, external penetration testing before launch, SOC 2 Type I then Type II, then ISO/IEC 27001.

Built on open standards

MCP and the MCP Registry format, A2A Agent Cards, OAuth and OpenID, W3C decentralised identifiers and credentials, ODRL agreements, Cedar policies, SCITT-style receipts, CVSS for findings, CloudEvents and OpenTelemetry. No lock-in: export everything.
